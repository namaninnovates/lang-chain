ABC Technologies - Company Policy Document

1. Refunds
- Annual subscription refunds: Customers can request a refund for their annual subscription within the first 30 days. These requests must be approved by a human supervisor.
- Monthly subscription refunds: Not permitted unless there was a billing error. All such requests require supervisor approval.

2. Subscription Cancellation
- Customers can cancel their subscription at any time. However, to prevent accidental churn, all cancellation requests must be reviewed and processed by a supervisor.

3. Account Closure
- Closing an account permanently deletes all user data. This action is irreversible and requires explicit human supervisor approval.

4. Compensation Requests
- Any request for compensation (e.g., due to service downtime) must be escalated to a supervisor for approval.

5. Escalation to Management
- If a customer explicitly requests to speak with a manager or supervisor, the request must be routed for human approval.
