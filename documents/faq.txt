ABC Technologies - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I activate my account?
A: An activation link is sent to your email upon registration. Click the link to activate your account.

Q: How can I reset my password?
A: You can reset your password by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login screen. You will receive an email with instructions.

Q: How do I update my profile details?
A: Go to your Account Dashboard and click on "Edit Profile" to update your name, email, or contact information.

Q: Where can I request an invoice?
A: Invoices are automatically generated and can be downloaded from the Billing section of your dashboard.

Q: How can I change my payment method?
A: Navigate to Settings -> Billing -> Payment Methods to add or remove credit cards.
