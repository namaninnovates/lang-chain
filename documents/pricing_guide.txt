ABC Technologies - Pricing Guide

We offer the following software subscription plans:

1. Basic Plan
- Cost: $29/month or $290/year
- Features: Core CRM functionality, up to 1,000 contacts, email support.

2. Professional Plan
- Cost: $79/month or $790/year
- Features: Everything in Basic, up to 10,000 contacts, priority email support, API access, advanced reporting.

3. Enterprise Plan
- Cost: Custom pricing starting at $299/month.
- Features: Unlimited contacts, 24/7 phone support, dedicated account manager, custom integrations.

Note: Annual plans offer a two-month discount compared to monthly billing.
